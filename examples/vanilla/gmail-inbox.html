Icon, tab title, and sound — all developer-controlled

live-favicon gives you three independent knobs — the icon, document.title, and a sound — and you decide what goes in each, per state you define. A new-mail notification is just one example below: a plain message shows its subject line in the tab title itself; a verification-code email shows the code instead, with a different icon and sound. The same three calls work for a chat DM, a shipped order, a finished build — anything you want visible without the user looking at the page.

Self-contained simulation — no real mailbox, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google/Gmail. It's here as a familiar example, not a mail integration.

1. Receive mail
